# Tin City Founders — Design Direction

## Three possible approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plateau Ledger | An editorial annual-report experience that frames local enterprise as a serious, investable movement. It feels measured, human, and internationally credible. | 0.07 |
| Studio Assembly | A bright, tactile workshop-inspired direction with modular cards, hands-on materials, and process-led storytelling. It highlights the craft of building businesses together. | 0.04 |
| Future Folk | A vivid cultural-tech blend where traditional visual patterns meet expressive digital intervention. It positions Jos as a surprising, creative innovation node. | 0.08 |

---

## Chosen approach: Plateau Ledger

### Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial annual report** meets **place-based development storytelling**. The website will borrow the restraint, considered typography, and evidence-first cadence of a well-designed foundation report, then animate it with the warmth and texture of a community on the Plateau.

### Core Principles

1. **Show the work before asking for support.** Programs, people, and delivery mechanisms take priority over abstract ambition.
2. **Be locally rooted and globally fluent.** The visual language must feel unmistakably connected to Jos while matching the trust signals expected by international institutions.
3. **Use contrast deliberately.** Deep evergreen and clay give the brand gravity; paper-like sand and wide whitespace keep the content open and legible.
4. **Make impact feel tangible.** Editorial photography, practical language, and clear partnership pathways replace generic claims and decorative excess.

### Color Philosophy

The base is a warm, paper-like **plateau sand** for openness and human approachability. A deep **Jos evergreen** carries institutional confidence and anchors the page. **Tin amber** enters sparingly as an ownable signal of momentum, opportunity, and the region's mining heritage. Clay and soft sage add material warmth without relying on fashionable gradients.

### Layout Paradigm

The page follows an **editorial field-journal rhythm**, rather than a centered stack of generic sections. Content moves between a full-bleed visual field, an offset impact column, a right-anchored proof rail, and staggered program panels. Every large section should feel like a new page in a living partnership dossier.

### Signature Elements

1. **The Plateau Rule:** an amber horizontal rule that begins at the left edge and recurs before major content shifts.
2. **Field Notes:** short uppercase locational labels such as “JOS, PLATEAU STATE” and “PARTNERSHIP BRIEF” that give every area documentary context.
3. **Layered paper planes:** slim sand, cream, and evergreen bands with barely visible grain, evoking a field ledger rather than a polished SaaS interface.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should be quiet confirmations of purpose. Links move with a short underline reveal; cards lift only a few pixels and expose an amber rule; navigation transforms into a usable solid panel once the visitor leaves the opening field. No gratuitous motion, no novelty scroll effects, and no effects that interrupt reading.

### Animation

Use a single 500ms entrance reveal for major text blocks only when motion is permitted: opacity from 0 to 1 and translation from 12px to 0 using `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. Stagger related items by 60ms. Button press feedback uses scale 0.97 over 140ms. Decorative rules can grow horizontally once per section. All movement must disable under reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** anchors key statements with a generous, assured editorial voice. **Manrope** handles body copy, navigation, labels, and buttons with clarity at small sizes. Headlines use 400-weight DM Serif with tight leading; small operational labels use Manrope 700 at 0.68–0.74em, uppercase, with tracked letters. Body text remains spacious and highly readable.

### Brand Essence

**Tin City Founders equips Jos-based founders to build durable enterprises, inviting international partners to turn local potential into shared economic progress.**

**Personality:** Grounded, enterprising, credible.

### Brand Voice

The voice is direct, specific, and evidence-oriented. Headlines should name the change and place; CTAs should describe the next meaningful action rather than ask people to “get started.”

> “Build with the people already building.”

> “Explore a partnership that reaches founders where growth begins.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The logo uses a **rising plateau / spark emblem**, a bold silhouette made from a stepped horizon and a single amber upward spark. In the website header, this mark sits alongside a bespoke, spaced wordmark treatment instead of a default-font brand name. The mark remains legible at favicon scale.

### Signature Brand Color

**Tin Amber — #D58C24.** It is reserved for active moments, partnership signals, and the Plateau Rule; its scarcity makes it recognizably Tin City Founders.
