# Tin City Founders Partnership Pitch Deck

**Audience:** Government institutions, development and international organizations, enterprise platforms, foundations, and values-aligned investors.

**Design direction:** Preserve the Plateau Ledger identity: deep evergreen, warm sand, Tin amber accents, DM Serif-style editorial headlines, clean Manrope-style supporting type, precise rules, and authentic Tin City Founders documentary photography. The tone is locally rooted, globally fluent, evidence-led, and quietly confident. Use generous negative space, exact editorial grids, and restrained motion. Avoid generic startup imagery, stock-business tropes, financial-return claims, and visual clutter.

## Cover

**Build with the people already building.**

Tin City Founders — a credible, community-led route into the entrepreneurial energy of Jos, Plateau State, Nigeria.

## Slide 1

### Opportunity is local before it is scalable.

*Jos has builders across technology, trade, creative work, agriculture, services, and informal enterprise.*

- The challenge is rarely ambition alone; it is fragmented access to peers, useful expertise, visibility, and opportunity.
- Tin City Founders makes local enterprise easier to see, connect, and strengthen.
- The proposition is not to start from zero. It is to help the people already building go further together.

## Slide 2

### No founder should build alone.

**Give before you take** is the community operating ethic.

- A trusted room where founders exchange practical insight, introductions, and support.
- A culture that values contribution before extraction and relationships before noise.
- A not-for-profit platform for entrepreneurship, innovation, and shared economic progress.

## Slide 3

### The community is a repeatable local engine.

- **Founder convenings** create a dependable rhythm for connection, peer exchange, and useful visibility.
- **Skills clinics and mentorship** respond to live business questions with practical expertise.
- **Give/Ask exchanges** surface concrete needs and turn goodwill into relevant introductions.
- **Built In Jos** recognizes founders across sectors and makes the region’s enterprise base more visible.

## Slide 4

### A live founder base is already taking shape.

**Built In Jos — current directory snapshot**

- **36** founder submissions received.
- **31** public profiles, visible with founder consent after duplicate checks.
- **30** ventures represented across **7** sectors and **4** locations.

*This is a consent-controlled community signal, not a claim of market coverage.*

## Slide 5

### What partners gain is credible local reach.

- A founder-dense environment with context that cannot be replicated through broad outreach alone.
- A practical route for expertise, tools, research, market access, and opportunity to reach builders who can use them.
- Co-designed activations shaped around a defined founder group and a real local need.
- A visible, locally credible presence earned by contributing something useful.

## Slide 6

### One community model can serve several institutional mandates.

| For | Partnership value |
|---|---|
| Government institutions | Inclusive local economic development, stronger entrepreneur visibility, and grounded ecosystem convening. |
| International organizations | Delivery and learning close to the people a programme is meant to serve. |
| Enterprise platforms and VCs | Founder readiness, thoughtful market access, and an informed view of emerging local operators. |
| Foundations and impact partners | Practical capability building and community infrastructure with measurable local signals. |

## Slide 7

### Three pathways turn intent into useful action.

**01 · Programme sponsorship**

Underwrite a founder cohort, learning series, or capability clinic designed around a defined local need.

**02 · Strategic collaboration**

Bring expertise, technology, market access, or research capacity into a community built for practical exchange.

**03 · Place-based investment**

Support local convening and collaborative solutions that help resilient small businesses stay and grow.

## Slide 8

### We design for evidence, not attention.

- A partner activation begins with a shared objective, target founder group, delivery owners, and evidence plan.
- Participation, founder registration growth, Give/Ask exchanges, introductions, stories, and engagement can become a clear learning narrative.
- Measures are agreed before activation, so reporting is useful to both the community and the partner.

> The goal is not logo placement. It is a useful intervention that founders can feel and partners can learn from.

## Slide 9

### Ninety days to first proof.

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Align** | Set the shared objective, founder profile, activation format, delivery owners, and evidence to capture. |
| **Activate** | Deliver the first useful touchpoint: a convening, capability clinic, spotlight run, or access-to-tools pathway. |
| **Learn** | Review participation, founder feedback, community requests, and partner-relevant signals. |
| **Build proof** | Document the first cycle and shape a renewal, deeper programme, or wider ecosystem collaboration. |

## Slide 10

### The partnership standard is simple.

**Grounded in local context. Designed for shared learning.**

- Tin City Founders brings the community context, trusted convening rhythm, and a practical activation agenda.
- Partners bring resources, expertise, access, or infrastructure that founders can genuinely use.
- Together, we leave the local enterprise community with stronger relationships, clearer next steps, and more durable opportunity.
