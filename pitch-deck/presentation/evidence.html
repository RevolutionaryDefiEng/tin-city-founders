08 · Evidence designUseful learning, agreed in advance

We design for evidence, not attention.

A partnership activation begins with a shared question and a visible pathway for learning—not a promise of headline numbers.

01
OBJECTIVE

Set the question.

Agree what the partner and community need the first activation to accomplish.

02
FOUNDER GROUP

Name who matters.

Define the entrepreneur group and local context that the activity is designed to serve.

03
ACTIVATION

Make it useful.

Choose a convening, clinic, access pathway, or spotlight that creates practical value.

04
SIGNALS

Learn clearly.

Agree participation, feedback, exchanges, introductions, and stories to capture.

Operating conviction

“The goal is not logo placement. It is a useful intervention that founders can feel and partners can learn from.”
