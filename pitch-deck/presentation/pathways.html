07 · Partnership pathways

Three ways to turn intent into useful action.

Begin with the partnership question, the founder group that matters, and the contribution that can make a practical difference.

01 · PROGRAMME SPONSORSHIP

Support a focused founder need.

Underwrite a founder cohort, learning series, or capability clinic designed around a defined local need.

Useful contributionLearning resources, expert time, access-to-tools, or focused programme support.

02 · STRATEGIC COLLABORATION

Bring a platform into the room.

Bring expertise, technology, market access, or research capacity into a community built for practical exchange.

Useful contributionTechnical expertise, partner pathways, knowledge sessions, or research collaboration.

03 · PLACE-BASED INVESTMENT

Strengthen the conditions for enterprise.

Support local convening and collaborative solutions that help resilient small businesses stay and grow.

Useful contributionConvening support, place-based visibility, local delivery capacity, or community infrastructure.
