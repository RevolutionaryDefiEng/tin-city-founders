09 · First-cycle activation

Ninety days to first proof.

A practical starting arc for a partner and the Tin City Founders community to test, learn, and decide what to build next.

WindowStageWhat happens
DAYS 01–15AlignSet the shared objective, founder profile, activation format, delivery owners, and evidence to capture.
DAYS 16–45ActivateDeliver the first useful touchpoint: a convening, capability clinic, spotlight run, or access-to-tools pathway.
DAYS 46–70LearnReview participation, founder feedback, community requests, and partner-relevant signals.
DAYS 71–90Build proofDocument the first cycle and shape a renewal, deeper programme, or wider ecosystem collaboration.

The right ninety-day plan is co-designed around the partnership question—not imposed as a template.
