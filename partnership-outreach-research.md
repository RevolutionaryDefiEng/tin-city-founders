# Tin City Founders — Partnership & Collaboration Outreach Research

**The first 30 institutions to reach out to, in priority order.**

Prepared: August 2026 · For: Tin City Founders (Jos, Plateau State, Nigeria)
Status: Early-stage, not-for-profit community association launched 2026.

---

## How to use this list

This is an **outreach priority list**, not just a directory. The 30 institutions are
ordered by how quickly and credibly a brand-new Jos community org can engage them —
starting with warm, local, low-cost wins and building toward higher-value, slower,
more formal relationships.

**Your core positioning in every conversation.** As a not-for-profit community
association you are rarely the *borrower* or the *direct grantee*. Your leverage is
that you **aggregate, prepare and mobilise the founders** these institutions want to
reach — tech founders, traders, students, and informal-sector operators (tailors,
mechanics, market women) across an under-served state. Frame outreach as:
*"We bring the member pipeline, local trust, venue and events in Plateau; you bring
the funding, tools, training or credibility."* That framing fits almost every entry
below.

**Three types of partner, don't confuse them:**
- **Org-level partners** — you join, register, or sign an MOU (networks, nonprofit
  tool programs, local hubs). These build *your* credibility and capacity.
- **Member-channel partners** — the money/benefit goes to individual founders; your
  role is to refer and prepare them (most bank, DFI and fellowship programs).
- **Sponsors** — they fund your events/programmes as CSR or brand visibility.

**Priority key:** 🟢 QUICK-WIN (0–6 months, low barrier) · 🟡 BUILD-TOWARD
(6–18 months, needs registration/track record) · 🔵 MEMBER-CHANNEL (refer members now,
no formal partnership needed).

**Before you reach out — three foundations to lay first** (these unlock the rest):
1. **Register the association** (CAC) — required for nonprofit tool programs, most
   grants, and credibility. SMEDAN offers free CAC registration (see #21).
2. **Stand up basic infrastructure** — a professional email domain, the website (now
   SEO-ready), and the "Built In Jos" directory as your evidence base.
3. **Start logging evidence from day one** — attendance, connections made,
   deals/Give–Ask exchanges, photos, testimonials. Every funder on this list will ask
   "show me your track record." Your first few events *are* your fundraising asset.

## TIER 1 — Local Jos & Plateau anchors *(reach out first: warm, local, foundational)*

These give you same-city credibility, venues, mentors, a talent pipeline, and your
first MOUs. Do these in person in the first month.

### 1. nHub Jos 🟢
- **What:** Northern Nigeria's first and oldest tech hub — incubation, tech training,
  mentorship, outsourcing. Confirmed active (10th anniversary + HackJos 2025, 500+
  participants).
- **Fit / mechanism:** Local hub collaboration — co-hosted events (HackJos),
  incubation referrals, shared training, mentor exchange. Your single most important
  local *peer*; partner, don't compete.
- **Shape:** MOU / co-host / mentor source.
- **Approach:** In person — 3rd Floor TAEN Business Complex, Old Airport Junction, Jos.
  Website: nhubnigeria.com *(verify URL)*; active on socials.
- **Why first:** Warm, local, immediately actionable, and a credibility signal for
  everyone else on this list.

### 2. PICTDA — Plateau State ICT Development Agency 🟢
- **What:** The state government's ICT agency (est. 2018, Jos). Runs **Code Plateau**
  bootcamps (1,785+ trained), a plan for **17 LGA innovation hubs**, and a new
  UNDP-partnered innovation hub.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Government program partnership — access to the LGA innovation
  hubs, Code Plateau talent pipeline, and state ecosystem events.
- **Shape:** MOU / named mobilisation partner.
- **Approach:** pictda.plateaustate.gov.ng — engage the agency directly for a
  partnership/MOU.
- **Priority:** Government alignment + venue/hub access on your doorstep.

### 3. PLATEC — Plateau Association of Technology Companies 🟢
- **What:** The collective voice of Plateau's tech businesses (est. 2018, 200+ members).
- **Fit / mechanism:** **Membership** (Startup / Corporate / Professional / Academic
  categories) plugs you straight into the organised local tech-company network.
- **Shape:** Paid membership.
- **Approach:** platec.org/membership · hello@platec.org · +234 703 617 1049.
- **Priority:** Cheap, high-trust local embedding.

### 4. Plateau State Government — Ministry of Science, Technology & Innovation 🟢
- **What:** The state's lead ministry for the innovation/digital-enterprise agenda.
  Ran the maiden **PLASSTIFEST** (Plateau Science, Technology & Innovation Festival,
  June 2026, Jos) and announced a Jos innovation hub at Plateau Polytechnic + an
  Agro-Innovation Makerspace (with UNDP).
- **Fit / mechanism:** Official community/event partner; mobilisation partner for the
  innovation hub; a channel for state youth-enterprise programmes. Directly
  overlapping mission, same city.
- **Shape:** MOU / official partner.
- **Approach:** plateaustate.gov.ng · ministry site plasmistin.com.ng. Best entry is an
  in-person meeting with the Commissioner. *(Verify the current Commissioner's name
  locally.)*
- **Priority:** Highest-value local government anchor. Make this an early call.

### 5. University of Jos — Centre for Entrepreneurship & Innovation 🟢
- **What:** Plateau's flagship university; its recently renamed entrepreneurship centre
  is explicitly open to "the wider community," with a ₦50m equipment upgrade and a
  scheme to fully fund viable student start-ups.
- **Fit / mechanism:** MOU; co-hosted training/incubation; mentor and venue sharing;
  student-founder pipeline into "Built In Jos."
- **Shape:** MOU / programme collaboration.
- **Approach:** unijos.edu.ng — approach the Centre director (Naraguta Campus) directly.
- **Priority:** Local, receptive, credibility-boosting. Pair with #4 as your two
  foundational Jos MOUs.

### 6. Plateau State University (PLASU), Bokkos — Entrepreneurship Development Centre 🟡
- **What:** State university whose EDC runs the ESP 300 programme and business
  incubation with a North-Central focus, open to "the entire Plateau citizens."
- **Fit / mechanism:** A second academic anchor extending reach beyond Jos city;
  incubation linkage for members.
- **Shape:** MOU / incubation collaboration.
- **Approach:** plasu.edu.ng/entrepreneurshipcenter.php.
- **Priority:** Useful for statewide expansion once the Unijos relationship is bedded in.

### 7. PLACCIMA / NACCIMA — Plateau State Chamber of Commerce 🟢
- **What:** Plateau's chamber of commerce (state affiliate of NACCIMA), a recognised
  private-sector convener linked with the Plateau Coalition of Business & Professional

- **Fit / mechanism:** Local business credibility and access to established SME
  owners/mentors — bridges your early founders to the incumbent Jos business community.
- **Shape:** Affiliate/associate membership or MOU.
- **Approach:** naccima.com · placobpa.org. *(Confirm the chamber's current name —
  appears as PLACCIMA and PLASCCIMA — and contact via NACCIMA/PLACOBPA or the Jos

## TIER 2 — Free org-level tools, credibility & recognition *(fast, self-serve wins)*

Once the association is registered, these are near-immediate wins that give you
professional infrastructure and perks to advertise to members.

### 8. Google for Nonprofits + TechSoup Nigeria 🟢

  YouTube nonprofit features; TechSoup validates nonprofits and channels software
  donations.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Ad Grants = free search advertising (a major visibility asset
  for a young org); Workspace runs your back office free.
- **Shape:** Free, for registered nonprofits.
- **Approach:** google.com/nonprofits · validate via nigeria.techsoup.global.
- **Priority:** Arguably your single highest-ROI early move. Requires CAC registration
  first.

### 9. GitHub for Nonprofits + Education 🟢
- **What:** Free/discounted GitHub for the org (Nonprofit Developer Pack) + free
  GitHub Education / Student Developer Pack (Pro + 100+ tools + Copilot) for student
  members.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Serves both the association and your student/tech cohort.
- **Shape:** Self-serve verification.
- **Approach:** github.com/solutions/industry/nonprofits · education.github.com.
- **Priority:** Quick self-serve win.

### 10. NITDA / Nigeria Startup Act — ecosystem-enabler registration 🟢
- **What:** The 2022 Startup Act's national portal (startup.gov.ng) formally recognises

  recognition and a route into government programmes; help member startups pursue the
  **Startup Label** (tax reliefs, funding access).

### 11. HubSpot for Startups + Notion for Startups (approved partner org) 🟢
- **What:** Deeper discounts (HubSpot up to 90% off; Notion up to 6 months free
  Business + AI) unlocked for startups **affiliated with an approved partner org**.

  extend the benefit to members — a concrete perk for your directory.
- **Shape:** Free partner application.
- **Approach:** hubspot.com/startups · notion.com/startups.
- **Priority:** Lightweight application, high perceived member value.

### 12. Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub 🔵
- **What:** Self-serve credits: $1k Azure at signup, up to $5k after verification,
  progressing toward up to $150k; plus GitHub Enterprise, M365, Azure/OpenAI access.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Onboard tech/software members immediately; run a signup workshop.
- **Shape:** Member benefit (founder applies individually; must own software IP, be a
  new Azure customer).
- **Approach:** microsoft.com/en-us/startups *(verify current landing)*.
- **Priority:** Member benefit you can activate this quarter.

### 13. AWS Activate 🔵🟡
- **What:** **Activate Founders** ($1k credits, self-serve) and **Activate Portfolio**
  (up to $100k, requires tie to an Activate Provider).
- **Fit / mechanism:** Members self-serve for Founders tier now; longer term, pursue
  **AWS Activate Provider** status so Tin City Founders can grant members the larger
  Portfolio credits.
- **Shape:** Member benefit now; org-level provider status later.
- **Approach:** aws.amazon.com/startups/credits.
- **Priority:** Founders tier = quick-win; Provider status = long-game.

## TIER 4 — Nigerian government & DFI member pipelines *(refer & mobilise members)*

The funding here goes to member businesses; your role is aggregation, referral and
preparation — a real, advertisable member benefit.

### 21. SMEDAN — free CAC registration + Select Programme 🟢
- **What:** Federal MSME agency. Runs **free business registration** for MSMEs via
  portal.smedan.gov.ng, the **SMEDAN Select Programme** (training, mentorship, market
  linkage, funding), and bank on-lending partnerships.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Run a **free-registration drive** for members — an immediate,
  concrete benefit for informal-sector/trade members, and it formalises the businesses
  feeding your directory.
- **Shape:** Aggregator/registration-drive partner; member benefit.
- **Approach:** smedan.gov.ng · portal.smedan.gov.ng.
- **Priority:** Early flagship member benefit. (Also unlocks Tier 2 nonprofit programs.)

### 22. Bank of Industry (BOI) — Plateau Youth Empowerment Fund 🟢
- **What:** Nigeria's largest development finance institution. There is a **live,
  Plateau-specific fund**: the state and BOI each committed ₦2bn (₦4bn total) for young
  Plateau entrepreneurs.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Be the beneficiary-mobilisation/referral partner for the state
  fund (accessed via your Tier 1 state-government relationship). Long term, explore
  becoming a BOI-appointed Business Development Service Provider.
- **Shape:** Referral pipeline (loans go to individual members).
- **Approach:** boi.ng — via the Plateau State Government relationship (#4), which
  co-administers the fund.
- **Priority:** Live, local, mission-aligned money for your members.

### 23. National Directorate of Employment (NDE) — Renewed Hope Employment Initiative 🟢
- **What:** Federal job-creation agency. **RHEI Phase 2** offers fully-funded
  vocational/digital/agri/enterprise training with business starter packs and equipment.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Strong for informal-sector, trade and student members; NDE needs
  local mobilisers — be the community partner for trainee recruitment and venue.
- **Shape:** Local mobilisation partner; member benefit (starter packs).
- **Approach:** nde.gov.ng · engage the **NDE Plateau State office** directly.
- **Priority:** Quick-win via the state office.

### 24. Nigerian Export Promotion Council (NEPC) — export-readiness training 🟢
- **What:** Federal export-development agency with offices in all 36 states; ran ~728
  trainings in 2025; runs the WEIDE fund for women exporters.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Co-host export-readiness training (they bring trainers, you bring
  participants/venue) for trade members and product-makers (agro, leather, fashion).
- **Shape:** Training co-host / MOU with the regional office.
- **Approach:** nepc.gov.ng · engage the **NEPC North-Central/Plateau office**.
- **Priority:** Quick-win for the export-curious subset of members.

## TIER 6 — International funders & flagship programmes *(channel members / build toward)*

The highest-prestige names. Mostly they fund *individuals* or work through
intermediaries — the realistic near-term play is to channel your members and build the
track record to partner directly later.

### 29. Tony Elumelu Foundation (TEF) — Entrepreneurship Programme 🔵
- **What:** Africa's leading entrepreneurship philanthropy. The **TEF Entrepreneurship
  Programme** gives US$5,000 non-refundable seed capital + training + mentorship +
  network; annual call (ran Jan–Mar 2026), open to businesses 0–3 years old, any
  African resident 18+.
- **Fit / mechanism:** The single most actionable opportunity for your *members*. Run
  **"TEF application clinics"** and become the org that gets Jos founders funded.
- **Shape:** Member channel (individual grant, 100% free to apply).
- **Approach:** Applications via tefconnect.com · info at tonyelumelufoundation.org.
- **Priority:** Watch the annual January call; channel members in.

### 30. Google for Startups — Black Founders Fund Africa + Cloud credits 🔵🟡
- **What:** **Black Founders Fund Africa** (up to $150k non-dilutive cash + Cloud
  credits for Black-founded startups); **Cloud Program** (up to $200k credits, $350k
  for AI); and a **Hub/Partner Network** for ecosystem orgs.
- **Fit / mechanism:** Channel qualifying "Built In Jos" founders to the Fund and Cloud
  credits; separately apply to the ecosystem Partner Network as a hub.
- **Shape:** Member channel (Fund/credits) + org-level partner network application.
- **Approach:** startup.google.com/programs/black-founders-fund/africa · startup.google.com.
- **Priority:** Cloud credits for members = quick-win; Fund + partner network = build-toward.

## Recommended outreach sequence

**Weeks 1–4 — Local foundations (in person):**
nHub Jos (#1) → Plateau State Ministry (#4) → University of Jos (#5) → PLATEC (#3) →
PICTDA (#2) → PLACCIMA (#7). Sign your first MOUs. Start the evidence log.

**Weeks 3–8 — Register + claim free wins (in parallel):**
SMEDAN free CAC registration (#21) → then Google for Nonprofits + Ad Grants (#8),
GitHub (#9), NITDA enabler registration (#10), HubSpot/Notion partner (#11), Startup
Grind Jos chapter (#14).

**Months 2–4 — Member benefit activations:**
Microsoft/AWS credits workshops (#12, #13), Ingressive for Good (#18), Fidelity SME
Forum (#27), and set up referral flows into TEF (#29), Access "W" (#26), BOI Plateau
fund (#22), NDE (#23), NEPC (#24), MTN Y'ellopreneur (#28), Google BFF (#30).

**Months 3–9 — Build-toward, after 2–3 documented events:**
AfriLabs membership (#15), ANDE West Africa (#16), UNDP timbuktoo (#17), CcHUB GATEWAY
(#19), PLASU Bokkos (#6), Sterling event sponsorship (#25), AWS Provider status (#13).

## Accuracy flags — verify before relying publicly

Program facts were checked against 2025–2026 sources; institution names, flagship
programme names and headline figures are drawn from official sources and reputable
reporting. The following need local/live confirmation before you cite them in
correspondence:

- **Exact current apply URLs** for Microsoft Founders Hub, Meta Llama Accelerator
  cohort, Ingressive for Good, Ventures Platform, and the nHub Jos website — main
  domains are stable, but sub-pages and open-call links shift.
- **Program open/close dates** (TEF Jan–Mar; Access "W" ~Sep–Oct; Google BFF windows) —
  annual calls move; confirm on the official portal before acting.
- **Current Plateau Commissioner for Science, Technology & Innovation** (two 2025 names
  found) and the exact current name/contact of **PLACCIMA/PLASCCIMA**.
- **Plateau innovation hub (Plateau Polytechnic)** and the **Agro-Innovation Makerspace
  (with UNDP)** — announced/under construction as of 2025–26; confirm operational status.
- **Women-only** programmes (screen members accordingly): Access "W", MTN Y'ellopreneur
  (also needs ~2yr trading history), NEPC WEIDE, GreenHouse Lab.
- **Paystack/Flutterwave** have **no confirmed structured community-partner program** —
  treat as sponsorship/BD/developer-community outreach only.
- **Not a direct grantor to a nonprofit:** DBN, BOI, SMEDAN, MTN, Access loans go to
  individual member businesses — your role is aggregation/referral, not borrower.
- **AfriLabs / ANDE fees & waivers** — confirm current membership cost and any
  emerging-market discounts directly.
